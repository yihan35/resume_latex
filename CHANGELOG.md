## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-09

### Added

- Local three-pane LaTeX resume editing with explicit save state.
- XeLaTeX compilation, first-page PDF preview, and SyncTeX source navigation.
- Filesystem confinement, sanitized process output, and loopback-only serving.
- Fictional sample resume, bilingual documentation, privacy automation, and
  public project governance.
